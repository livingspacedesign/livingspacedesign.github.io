Small Bathroom Storage

Small Bathroom Organisation Ideas Under $30

Intro

A small bathroom gets messy fast. Toothpaste, creams, hair tools, medicines — everything ends up on the same small sink, and the room looks cluttered even when it is clean. The fix is not a bigger bathroom. It is a place for everything, and a habit of putting it back.

This guide has 12 bathroom organisation ideas, all under $30 in total. Each one lists what it costs, and none of them needs drilling. Most are renter-friendly.

The whole plan fits in one shopping trip: a tray, a set of hooks, two baskets and a pack of labels. Buy those four things, spend one hour putting them in place, and the bathroom will stay organised for months — as long as the five-minute weekly reset becomes a habit.

1. A tray on the sink — $5–$8

A small tray on the sink holds the toothbrush holder, the soap and the hand cream in one neat group. Everything in one place, nothing scattered, and the tray lifts off when you clean the sink. A tray with raised edges stops bottles tipping over.

2. Glass jars for cotton pads and buds — $4–$7

Two small glass jars — one for cotton pads, one for cotton buds — look tidy and make it obvious when you are running low. They cost a few dollars, and they turn a messy pile into a display. Keep them away from the tap so they stay dry.

3. Hooks on the back of the door — $4–$7

Stick-on hooks on the back of the bathroom door hold a bathrobe, a towel, and a small bag. The door was empty space — now it works. Use strong adhesive hooks, and keep heavy items off them.

4. Labels on every box — $2–$4

A label on every box and basket means everyone in the flat puts things back in the right place. A pack of chalk labels costs a few dollars, and labelled boxes stay tidy far longer than unlabelled ones.

5. Drawer dividers for the vanity — $5–$8

Small dividers in the vanity drawer split it into sections — one for hair tools, one for creams, one for medicines. No more digging through a mixed drawer. A set of adjustable dividers costs about $6 and fits most drawers.

6. An over-door rack for towels — $8–$12

An over-door rack holds two or three towels without a single hole in the wall. It hangs on the door, and it moves with you when you leave. It is the best fix for a bathroom with no towel rail.

7. A suction caddy in the shower — $5–$8

A suction caddy sticks to the shower tiles and holds shampoo, conditioner and soap at hand height. No drilling, no damage, and it comes off for cleaning. Check the suction cups monthly — they loosen in steam.

8. Small baskets under the sink — $6–$10

Two small baskets under the sink hold cleaning supplies in one and spare toiletries in the other. Baskets keep the bottles upright and easy to pull out. Label them so nobody mixes them up.

9. A toothbrush holder that hangs — $4–$6

A toothbrush holder that hangs on the wall or the mirror keeps the sink clear and the brushes dry. A stick-on holder costs a few dollars and frees the whole sink edge. Rinse it weekly so it stays clean.

10. A makeup bag, not a makeup pile — $5–$8

One small makeup bag, kept in a drawer, holds everything you use daily. When you are done, the bag goes back in the drawer and the sink is clear. A bag beats a pile — a pile spreads, a bag contains.

11. Rolled towels in a basket — $5–$8

Roll spare towels and stack them in a basket on a shelf or on the floor. Rolled towels take up less space than folded ones, and they look like a hotel display. One basket holds three or four towels.

12. A weekly five-minute reset — $0

Once a week, five minutes: wipe the sink, put everything back in its tray or basket, and take out one thing you have not used in a month. A small bathroom only stays organised if someone resets it — and five minutes is all it takes.

Conclusion

A small bathroom is not hard to organise — it just needs a tray, a few hooks, and a habit. The tray on the sink, the hooks on the door, and the baskets under it together cost under $30, and they can make the room feel twice as big. Start with the tray, add the hooks, and keep the five-minute reset.

Budget recap

- Tray + jars: ~$12

- Door hooks + over-door rack: ~$15

- Drawer dividers + labels: ~$8

Total for a fully organised bathroom: ~$30 — right on budget.
