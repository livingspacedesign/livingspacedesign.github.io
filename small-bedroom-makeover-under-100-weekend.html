Small Bedroom Makeover Under $100: One Weekend

Intro

A bedroom makeover does not need a contractor or a big budget. With $100 and one weekend, you can declutter, swap the bedding, refresh one wall and fix the lighting — and the room will feel like a different place on Monday morning. The trick is a plan: Saturday for the hard work, Sunday for the finishing touches.

This guide is a 2-day plan with 12 steps. Each step has a price, and the whole weekend stays under $100. Most steps are free; the money goes on bedding, cushion covers and one or two small swaps.

1. Saturday morning: empty the room — $0

Take everything small out of the room — cushions, throws, books, ornaments, cables. Put it all on the dining table. An empty room shows you what you actually have, and it makes the next steps ten times easier. This takes about 30 minutes, and it is the most important step of the weekend.

2. Declutter in three boxes — $0

Three boxes: keep, donate, toss. Be honest — if you have not used it in six months, it goes. Small bedrooms suffer most from clutter, and this step alone can make the room feel bigger. One box out the door is worth $50 of new decor.

3. Move the bed first — $0

Try the bed on the longest wall, or pushed into a corner to free up floor space. The bed is the biggest piece, so its position decides everything else. Move it, lie down, and check the view from the door before you commit. This costs nothing and changes the room more than any purchase.

4. Wash the windows and clean the mirror — $0

Wash the windows inside and out, and clean any mirror until it shines. Light is free, and dirty glass blocks more of it than you think. A clean window can make a dark bedroom feel a whole shade brighter, and it costs only 20 minutes.

5. One accent wall, one litre of paint — $18–$25

Saturday afternoon: paint the wall behind the bed a soft colour — sage, dusty blue or warm terracotta. One litre covers one wall. Tape the edges, use two thin coats, and let it dry overnight. This is the single biggest visual change of the weekend.

6. Swap the bedding — $20–$30

New bedding in one calm colour — white, cream, or pale sage — changes the whole room, because the bed is the biggest surface in it. A new duvet set costs $20–$30 and does more than any other single purchase. Wash it first, so it smells fresh on Sunday night.

7. Swap the cushion covers — $12–$18

New cushion covers in two colours that match the accent wall — for example terracotta and cream. Covers are cheaper than new cushions, and they change the whole bed. Keep the old covers in a bag in case you want them back.

8. Add one throw and fold it properly — $12–$15

A throw in a warm neutral, folded over the foot of the bed, not draped everywhere. One throw is enough — two throws look messy in a small room. A chunky knit or a linen throw both work, and the throw is the first thing you see when you walk in.

9. Sunday morning: fix the lighting — $10–$15

One warm bedside lamp, or a string of fairy lights along the headboard, changes how the room feels at night. Warm light is relaxing, and a lamp at bed height makes the room feel cosy. Check the ceiling bulb too — swap it for a warm one for about $3.

10. Rearrange the shelves — $0

Put back only what you love. Books in groups of three, one plant per shelf, and one empty shelf if you can manage it. Empty space is part of the design — a shelf that is 20% empty looks calm, a shelf that is full looks crowded.

11. One plant, one picture — $10–$15

Sunday afternoon: one small plant on a shelf ($5–$8) and one framed picture or print on the accent wall ($5). Two things, one each — do not buy more, or the room tips back into clutter. A plant adds life, and a picture makes the wall feel finished.

12. The 10-minute Sunday reset — $0

Finish by setting a weekly habit: every Sunday, 10 minutes — make the bed properly, fluff the cushions, clear one surface, take out one thing you do not need. A makeover only lasts if you maintain it, and 10 minutes a week is all it takes.

Conclusion

The weekend works because the order matters: declutter first, move the bed second, paint third, and buy last. If you buy first, you decorate around clutter. Under $100, the room gets a new wall, new bedding, new cushions, a throw, a plant and warm light — and the biggest change, the declutter, cost nothing at all.

Budget recap

Paint (1 litre): ~$20

Duvet set: ~$25

Cushion covers (2): ~$15

Throw: ~$13

Lamp + plant + picture: ~$20

**Total: ~$93 — $7 left over, and a bedroom that feels new on Monday morning.**
